# Paid beta validation

## Target user

A developer, creator or hybrid worker with a Windows desktop and a Mac at the
same desk who wants to keep one keyboard, mouse and audio output.

## Offer

- Founders Early Access: EUR 19 one-time, first 100 licenses.
- Full functionality; clearly labelled beta limitations.
- One person, up to three owned devices; 30-day refund.
- No subscription until the product provides an ongoing hosted service.

## Go/no-go sample

Recruit 20 target users. Continue toward a public v1 when at least 70% can pair
without help in under five minutes, at least half use the product three days per
week after two weeks, and at least five complete a real EUR 19–29 purchase.

Do not prioritize Linux, remote desktop, cloud accounts, enterprise fleet
management or reverse audio before this test. Candidate follow-ups are
bidirectional input, multi-device layouts and file transfer, chosen from usage
and interview evidence.
